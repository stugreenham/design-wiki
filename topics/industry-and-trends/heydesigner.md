# HeyDesigner

## What it is

A hand-picked list of design links, posted daily on the site and sent weekly by email, running since 2012. It covers UX, modern CSS, design systems, AI tools and product thinking, and has 10,000+ readers. It's also available by RSS.

## When I'd use it

A quick daily or weekly scan to stay across what the design community is writing about. A good source of links for this wiki.
