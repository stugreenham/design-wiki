# Design Better

## What it is

A long-running podcast and Substack newsletter, hosted by Eli Woolery and Aarron Walter, about design, technology and the creative process. Guests come from across creative fields, from product design leaders to architects and artists. It has 260,000+ subscribers and has been recognised by the Webbys.

Alongside the interviews it publishes:
- **The Roundup:** the key lessons from each episode, written up
- **The Brief:** practical guides, often on working with AI

## Recent episodes worth a listen (autumn 2026)

- Randy Hunt (Notion, Etsy) on why the best design teams stay small
- Blaine Billingsley (OpenAI) on the collaboration problem AI hasn't solved yet
- The Brief: 7 principles for better AI collaboration
- The Brief: the essential setup for building web apps with AI

## When I'd use it

Staying across where design is heading, and hearing how experienced design leaders think. Good for commutes.
