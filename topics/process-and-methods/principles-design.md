# Design Principles library

## What it is

A free library of 1,600+ real world design principles from 200+ organisations and people, including the NHS, Monzo, Clearleft and Amber Case's Principles of Calm Technology. It also has short articles on writing good principles, like "Why Most Design Principles Fail" and "The Difference Between Rules and Principles".

## When I'd use it

Writing or refreshing principles for a team or design system, and wanting real examples to start from.
