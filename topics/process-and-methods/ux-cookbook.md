# The UX Cookbook

## What it is

Short "recipes" for UX methods, like A/B testing, card sorting and usability testing. They're grouped into content strategy, data and analytics, prototyping and visual design, and research and testing. It also has a pocket recipe for designing accessible websites.

## When I'd use it

Planning a method I haven't run in a while, or sharing a simple how-to with non-designers.
