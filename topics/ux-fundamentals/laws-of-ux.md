# Laws of UX

## What it is

A well-known collection of about 30 psychology-based principles for designing interfaces, each with a short explanation, the origin and practical tips. Also available as a book, cards and posters.

## Some of the laws

- **Hick's Law:** more choices make decisions slower
- **Fitts's Law:** bigger, closer targets are quicker to hit
- **Jakob's Law:** people expect your site to work like the ones they already use
- **Miller's Law:** people hold about 7 items in working memory
- **Tesler's Law:** some complexity can't be removed, only moved
- **Peak-End Rule:** people judge an experience by its best or worst moment and its end
- **Doherty Threshold:** responses under 400ms keep people engaged

## When I'd use it

Explaining or backing up a design decision with an established principle, or as a quick refresher.
