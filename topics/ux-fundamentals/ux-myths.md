# UX Myths

## What it is

A collection of 34 common UX misconceptions, each explained with research and articles showing why it doesn't hold up. Older (2010 to 2015) but still very relevant.

## Some of the myths

- People don't scroll
- All pages should be reachable in 3 clicks
- Choices should always be limited to 7 plus or minus 2
- People can tell you what they want
- If it works for Amazon, it will work for you
- You are like your users
- Accessible sites are ugly
- Simple means minimal

## When I'd use it

Pushing back on a stakeholder assumption with evidence.
