# Growth.Design: Cognitive biases and principles

## What it is

A list of 100+ cognitive biases and design principles that shape how people use products, each with examples and tips. It groups them by the four steps people go through: filtering information, finding meaning, acting in limited time, and remembering. There's also a downloadable cheat sheet.

## Examples

- **Confirmation bias:** people look for evidence that confirms what they already think
- **Anchoring:** the first number people see shapes how they judge the rest
- **Decoy effect:** a third, worse option makes another look better
- **Default bias:** people tend to stick with the pre-selected option

## When I'd use it

Understanding why users behave a certain way, or spotting biases a flow could work with or against.
