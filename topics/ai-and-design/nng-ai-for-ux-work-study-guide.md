# AI for UX Work: Study Guide

## Summary

A curated reading list of NN/g's articles, videos and podcasts on using AI in UX work, sorted by discipline. The stance is "yes, use AI, but think critically". Treat AI like a talented intern that still needs supervision, and stay the strategist directing the work.

## Key takeaways

- **The process isn't dead, it's compressed.** Designers still move through the same steps, only faster
- **Roles are shifting** towards defining user needs, intent and journeys, and away from pushing pixels. The UX generalist is coming back
- **Curating context for AI** is becoming a core design output ("UX context design"), alongside critique and clear evaluation criteria
- **Research:** AI helps most with planning and analysis. Synthetic users and AI moderated interviews can supplement real research but not replace it. Don't outsource the learning
- **Writing:** give AI real example copy and ask for options, rather than just tone words
- **Prompting:** the CARE structure (context, ask, rules, examples) and precise visual references beat vague prompts
- **Taste and judgment** are what keep designers valuable as more people can create
