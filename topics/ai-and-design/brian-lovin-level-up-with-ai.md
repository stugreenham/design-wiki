# Brian Lovin: How to level up with AI as a designer

## Summary

A Dive Club interview with Brian Lovin, a designer at Notion, about using AI tools in design work and helping build AI tools at Notion. It covers Brian's changing tool stack, coding with AI, a prototyping setup, and how designers can stay relevant.

## Key takeaways

- **AI as a design material:** designing AI products, not just using AI to design
- **A prototyping playground:** Brian built a shared space at Notion where designers prototype in code
- **The tool stack keeps changing:** back to Cursor, plus Conductor for coding, and Agentation and DialKit for front end work
- **Practical prompting tips** for getting better results from AI
- **"AI is not magic":** a grounded view of what it can and can't do
- **Career strategy** for designers in an AI world
