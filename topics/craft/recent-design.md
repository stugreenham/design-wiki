# Recent

## What it is

A design inspiration site collecting recent work across web, interface, branding, motion, illustration, posters and print. It also has sections for launch videos, OG images, app screenshots and app icons, plus a design jobs board.

## When I'd use it

A broad visual scan for fresh ideas. Anything worth keeping visually goes into Eagle.
