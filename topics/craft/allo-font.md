# Allo

## What it is

A free, open source font family (OFL licence) by Josh Puckett, described as "your friendly neighborhood typeface". It's designed to feel warm without being too cute, and clear without being too cold, and it's tuned for screens and interfaces.

## Highlights

- **Three variable axes:**
  - **Weight:** 100 to 900
  - **Roundness:** 0 to 100
  - **Optical size:** 14 to 32, adjusting automatically between text and display sizes
- **Real obliques,** drawn rather than just slanted
- **Interface details:** tabular figures, fractions, punctuation that lifts beside capitals, and arrows that appear when you type `->`
- **Built-in icons and symbols,** like ⌘, ★, ♥ and ✓
- **Stylistic sets and alternates,** for example a single-story "a"
- **50+ languages,** 600+ glyphs

## When I'd use it

A friendly, free interface font for a product, prototype or side project, especially where a rounder, softer feel suits the brand.
