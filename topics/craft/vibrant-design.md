# Vibrant Design

## What it is

A daily inspiration site of websites, page sections, launch videos, OG images and UI shots. Its standout feature is browsing by **section type**: hero, features, bento grid, pricing, CTA, logo cloud, testimonials, FAQ, footer, docs, login and sign up, 404 and more. It also has moodboards and a "surprise me" button.

## When I'd use it

Designing one specific section of a page, like a pricing table or FAQ, and wanting lots of real examples of just that section.
