# Interfaces: design engineering magazine

## What it is

A design engineering magazine by Jakub Krehel about what makes an interface feel great: animation, typography, layout and colour. Articles use live, interactive examples that show "recommended" versus "not recommended" side by side.

## When I'd use it

Sharpening visual and interaction craft, and explaining small polish details to engineers.
