# State of UX Portfolios 2026

## Summary

UXfolio surveyed 100+ hiring decision makers, mostly design leads and directors, on what gets a UX portfolio noticed and shortlisted. The short version: the portfolio matters more than your CV, it should be a website, reviewers scan fast, and your thinking matters more than polish. The full report is free but needs an email signup. These notes come from the public summary.

## Key takeaways

- **The portfolio beats experience.** 78% rely on it most to judge fit, ahead of industry experience (46%), CV (40%) and past employers (12%)
- **Make it a website.** 72% prefer a portfolio site over Figma prototypes (30%), PDFs (27%) or slides (26%). A Google Drive folder goes to the back of the pile
- **The first pass is a scan, not a read.** "If I cannot find the projects within the first 30 seconds, I reject it immediately"
- **AI isn't screening portfolios.** 71% say their organisation doesn't use AI to review them. A person is still reading
- **Your thinking is what matters.** Strong case studies show reasoning, not just final screens
- **Use AI as a tool, not the author.** Reviewers notice, and being open about how you used it builds trust. "AI doesn't reduce the value of portfolios, it raises the bar for them"
