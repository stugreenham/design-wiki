# The Strategic Product Designer: resources and people to follow

## Summary

Vitaly Friedman shares "The Strategic Product Designer", a Notion hub by Daniel Andor. It's a curated set of books, articles, courses and newsletters to help designers move towards UX strategy: business, strategy tools, product knowledge, growth and experimentation. Vitaly's point is that designers are great at pixels, but good pixels come from strategic decisions, and one way to get there is to measure the outcome of UX work.

## Key takeaways

- **Be strategic about decisions,** not just good at screens
- **Measure the outcome of UX work** to show its impact on the business
- **From the comments:** shipping is easy to measure, but proving you made something better is the hard part. Concrete numbers (like cutting a task from a minute to 15 seconds) are what convince the business
