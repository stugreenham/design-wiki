# UX Interview Coach

## What it is

A free AI practice tool by Sabrina Staniewska. Pick a topic, optionally paste in a job brief, answer an interview question, and it scores your answer and suggests what a real panel would ask next. You can email yourself the session.

## Its best tip

"Name the decision, say what you rejected, and defend the choice. A list of steps reads junior. A defended fork reads senior."

## When I'd use it

Practising how I talk about design decisions before an interview. It says itself that it's no substitute for a mock interview with real people.
