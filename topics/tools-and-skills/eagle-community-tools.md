# Eagle Community: Tools

## What it is

A community-submitted directory of design tools and websites from the Eagle app's community. You can filter by field (UI, graphic, illustration, game, interior), by category (AI tools, inspiration, colours, icons, fonts, mockups, stock images and more) and by price (free, partly free, paid).

## When I'd use it

Hunting for a tool or resource of a certain kind, especially free ones.
