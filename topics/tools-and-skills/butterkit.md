# ButterKit

## What it is

A native Mac app for making App Store screenshots. You drop app screenshots onto realistic 3D device frames, design the layout, auto-translate the screenshots and store text into all 50 App Store languages, then upload straight to App Store Connect. AI agents can drive it through its MCP.

## When I'd use it

Preparing or refreshing App Store screenshots for an iOS app, especially in several languages.
