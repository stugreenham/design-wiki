# Karl Koch's design engineering skills

## What it is

A free collection of AI agent skills based on Karl Koch's writing on design engineering, fluid interfaces, semantic HTML and judging AI output. There are around 20 skills, from `fluid-design` and `subtractive-design` to `ai-output-judgement` and `hiring-manager-bar` (a portfolio review). It also has Figma Design and Make versions.

## Key ideas

- **One master skill, `dxe`,** runs a full design engineering review of a project in five phases: intent, platform, feel, bridge (design to code) and shipping
- **Use single skills for a narrow lens,** or `dxe` for the full pass. It only loads the specialist skills a project actually needs
- **Native first:** use real platform controls and semantic HTML before building custom ones
- **Hardening prototypes:** `prototype-to-production` checks the empty, error, auth and focus states that AI built prototypes usually skip
- **Built on essays** worth reading on their own, like [Write-first design](https://karlkoch.me/writing/write-first-design), [The slop isn't the models](https://karlkoch.me/writing/the-slop-isnt-the-models) and [10 Principles for Fluid UI](https://karlkoch.me/writing/10-principles-for-fluid-ui)

## When I'd use it

Reviewing something built with AI before it ships, or getting a coding agent to care about motion, affordances and polish.
