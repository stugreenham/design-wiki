# Playground

## What it is

An AI design tool for quick graphics. Start from a blank design, upload an image to edit, or pick a template for things like logos, social posts, posters, T-shirts, mockups and wallpapers.

## When I'd use it

Making quick marketing or social graphics without opening a full design tool.
